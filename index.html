QR Table Check-In & Instant POS Sync for Luxury Retail Showroom

Replacing awkward manual paperwork and glitchy spreadsheets with seamless tabletop VIP registration and instant POS sync.

💡 The Project Summary

In a luxury jewelry showroom, closing a high-ticket sale should feel prestigious—not like doing paperwork at the post office. Sales consultants were getting bogged down manually collecting international tourist passports, phone numbers, and flight dates for Australia’s 10% tax-free export scheme. Their early workaround—a fragile patchwork of an online form, Google Sheets, and no-code connectors—was constantly glitching, losing buyer records, and stalling consultations during busy hours. We replaced that broken chain with elegant acrylic QR stands on every viewing table. While admiring gems, clients scan to register their lifetime guarantee in 20 seconds. By the time the consultant prepares the invoice, the client's profile and tax-free status are already active in the Cin7 POS.

❌ BEFORE: Glitchy Workarounds & Broken Sales Momentum
  • •
    Fragile, Glitching Patchwork: An earlier makeshift setup (form app + Google Sheets + webhooks) constantly froze, broke data sync, and dropped customer contact records.
  • •
    Awkward Admin Kills VIP Mood: Consultants had to spend 5 minutes manually retyping foreign addresses, phone numbers, and passport details instead of focusing on the sale.
  • •
    Consultants Tied Up During Surges: When multiple private groups arrived, staff were trapped doing administrative data entry rather than attending to prospective clients.
  • •
    Manual Tax Errors: Staff had to manually verify flight departure dates and calculate Australia's 10% Tourist Refund Scheme (TRS) discounts by hand.
✅ AFTER: Prestigious VIP Experience & Flawless POS Sync
  • •
    Effortless Tabletop Registration: Clients scan a branded acrylic QR stand on their viewing table to activate their lifetime guarantee in 20 seconds.
  • •
    Sub-2-Second POS Readiness (< 1.4s): The profile and tax-free status appear in Cin7 POS before the consultant even opens the invoice screen.
  • •
    100% Reliable Cloud Architecture: Custom Google Cloud Run microservice handles multiple simultaneous showroom registrations with zero lag or dropped records.
  • •
    Automated 10% Tax Exemption: Departure dates are automatically verified within the 60-day Australian tax window and tagged GST-Free in Cin7.

WHY SERVERLESS BEATS NO-CODE

  • • Instant Auto-Scaling: Handles busy showroom surges with zero lag, unlike Make/Zapier which time out or drop records on sudden spikes.
  • • Local Sydney Deployment: Hosted in Google Cloud's Sydney datacenter (`australia-southeast1`) ensuring blistering 12ms network roundtrips.
  • • Asynchronous Staff Alerts: Internal notifications (Slack floor alerts, manager emails) run in the background so customer POS checkout is never delayed.
💎

BOTTOM-LINE BUSINESS IMPACT

  • • Protected VIP Luxury Ambiance: Sales consultants remain 100% focused on consultation and closing without awkward administrative interruption.
  • • Automated 10% Tax Exemption: Pre-qualifies departing international visitors under Australia's Tourist Refund Scheme (TRS) without cashier math errors.
  • • Saved $4,000+ per Year: Replaced bloated monthly SaaS subscriptions (Tally, Make, Mailgun) with a custom microservice running 100% inside GCP's free tier.
🛡️

STORE DATA SECURITY & INTEGRITY

  • • Smart VIP Deduplication: Intelligently matches existing buyers by phone or email. Never creates messy duplicate accounts or overwrites VIP purchase histories.
  • • Spam & Bot Shield: Built-in rate limiters and honeypot traps prevent internet scrapers from spamming the form or overloading store databases.
  • • Bank-Grade Encryption: All store POS credentials and API keys are stored in encrypted Google Secret Manager with strict access controls.
